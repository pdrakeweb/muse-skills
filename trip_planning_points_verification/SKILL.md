# Trip Planning Points Stay Verification

Repository: https://github.com/pdrakeweb/muse-skills/tree/main/trip_planning_points_verification


## Verify the booking window before quoting a rate

- Compute the months between today and the travel dates from the calendar dates before you describe how far away the trip is.

- Check whether the live booking calendar is open for the exact travel dates before you quote a points rate for those dates.

- Say that no live price exists when the calendar is not open for those dates, and do not quote a rate from a different date range as the rate for those dates.

- Carry the exact dates, party size and room occupancy into every live check, and record the date you checked.

## Confirm the property identity before quoting its rate

- Verify the current brand and name of the property from a current source before you quote its points rate.

- Check for a rebrand, closure or reopening with a dated source when a property name matches more than one property or has changed over time.

- Do not carry a rate from a similarly named property to the property you are reporting.

- Verify the location of the property you are reporting, and do not rely on a name match alone.

## Verify the points tier and party pricing

- Read the current points tier chart and record its publication date and any reshuffle date before you quote a per night rate.

- Quote the per night rate for the exact party size and room type, and check whether extra guests change the points price.

- Do not quote a flat rate from an older chart when the current chart prices the property in a tier or range.

- Label a rate as unverified when you could not confirm it against a current tier chart or a live calendar.

## Verify program features and credit terms

- Check that a program feature exists for the exact program, card and booking channel before you say it works.

- Read the current terms for any credit you quote, including the amount, the booking channel, any minimum stay or prepaid condition and the date window.

- Do not present a feature or a credit as available when the source you checked covers a different channel or a different product.

- Make any recommendation that depends on an unverified feature or credit conditional, and say what is still unverified.

## Alternatives for better results

- Ask the hotel or the program directly when the live calendar is phone only, and record the answer with the date you asked.

- Compare a cash rate for the same dates when the points rate is unverified, so the user can judge the trip without a points figure that is not established.
